# Native conditional PAPER v1
Existing PAPER admission only supports its own executable strategy schema. Native research engines (coverage-1, coverage-2 / Foundry, coverage-combinations-v1 and coverage-indicators-v1) now have a separate durable research-only observation stage.

* Every dual-positive historical hypothesis is identified by engine version, asset and immutable strategy ID. The original discovery and validation results are screened for positive net returns, no ruin, max 25% drawdown in each period, at least six entries combined and at least two per period. Terminal outcomes retain a reason. Missing evidence is not a failed backtest.
* Full records are recovered from verified compressed archives using the permanent identity and original provenance. Missing archives and data stay pending. Legacy archives are read one bounded record at a time (8 MiB per record, 768 MiB total expansion); the previous 64 MiB expansion-limit failure is eligible for an immediate retry. SHA256 and full record-count checks remain mandatory. The original source, spec hash, policy and return evidence are checked; the coverage engine also has an independently implemented scalar replay. Native return paths are verified arithmetically; this is not an independent broker fill model.
* The precise native executor repeats the fixed rules on real eligible bars using the original venue/provider and cost model, including indicators, event calendars, combination logic, position/risk rules and weekly aggregation. Recheck requires the original discovery/validation screen to survive and three disjoint chronological folds: at least two positive, combined positive net, no ruin, drawdown at most 25%, at least six total entries. Undefined input history is not counted as a failure. These historical checks are NOT independent prospective evidence or the central ACCEPT gate.
* Qualified native hypotheses are admitted to a distinct conditional queue (12 crypto, 20 US, 16 BIST, at most 3 per asset). Qualified candidates waiting for capacity are retained; terminal failures do not re-enter automatic screening. Candidates with unavailable source/history/calendar are retried as data becomes available. Each cycle has bounded archive recovery (six archives), native screening (192 candidates or 105 seconds), and prioritizes existing observations before new screening; the existing single-asset coverage focus remains unchanged.
* Registration freezes code, data prefix, calendar prefix and costs. Only bars formed strictly after registration count toward 90 native bars; a weekly strategy requires 90 subsequent complete weeks. Coverage open-to-open accounting needs the next open. Interim net metrics use native marked/liquidated-at-end accounting and are provisional, not actual orders. Two consecutive identical integrity mismatches pause the observation without altering previous evidence.
* Completion requires positive net return, no ruin, at most 25% drawdown and at least six entries on the forward period. The conditional result neither promotes to ordinary PAPER nor authorizes LIVE. Short borrow remains an assumption and the research is not a broker simulation. Existing legacy PAPER, statistical controls, and sealed Foundry cohorts remain separate.
* The dashboard publishes counts, reason distributions, the current observation list, and candidate decisions (first 5,000 in the feed, full identities in the checkpoint). Archive recovery and screening cycle errors are explicitly reported.
